How Bifurca works

The ride climbs toward the split at 3.00x. About 1 ride in 3 gets there; the rest go down on the way.

At the split you get 4 seconds: take 3.00x, or pick a road.

Smooth road: keeps climbing like a normal ride until it goes down.

Wild road: big ramp jumps. Each jump has a 1 in 3 chance to land. Land it and your multiplier triples (9x, 27x, 81x…). Miss and it's over. Cash out between jumps.

Odds in plain words: every choice pays back 97 of every 100 credits bet on average. About 1 ride in 3 reaches the split. A Wild jump lands 1 time in 3.
